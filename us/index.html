Who you'll work with

Velorien is run by Hitin Banga, an independent web and UI/UX designer based in Dubai. You deal directly with the person designing and building your site.

How much does a website cost?

Our business websites start at $1,499 for a Starter landing page, $2,999 for a 4-page Professional website and $5,499 for a fully custom Premium website. Website Care is $149 per month and extra pages are $150 each. All prices are in US dollars.

How long does a website take?

Starter takes 7 days, Professional 10–14 days and Premium 14–21 days. The clock starts once we have your content and key details, so sending them early is the fastest way to launch.

You’re based in Dubai. How does working together work?

Everything runs remotely over email, video calls and WhatsApp, the same way we work with every client. You deal directly with Hitin, the person designing and building your site, from the first call to launch.

What do I need to provide?

Your logo if you have one, the services or products you offer, your contact details and any photos you want used. Not sure what you have? Send us a quick email and we’ll point out what’s missing.

Are domain and hosting included?

Domain and hosting fees are paid separately to the provider. We can advise on what to buy and help connect it to your website at launch.

How does payment work?

Prices are in US dollars. We agree the payment schedule with you before any work starts, typically a deposit to begin and the balance at launch.

Can I ask for changes?

Yes. Revisions are part of the design and build stages, so you see the work take shape and can steer it. The exact scope is confirmed when we agree your package.

Can I add more pages later?

Yes. Extra pages are available from $150 each, or you can move up to a larger package. Starter is a single landing page with your services, so many clients add pages as they grow.

What happens after launch?

You get a finished website and a clear handover. If you’d like it kept updated, backed up and monitored, Website Care is available at $149 per month. It’s optional.

Can you add an AI chat assistant to my website?

Yes. It’s the same kind of assistant you can try on this page: it answers your customers 24/7 in their language and sends you their name and contact details. Setup is $699, then $79 per month, or $59 per month if you have Website Care. Setup is included with the Premium Website.
