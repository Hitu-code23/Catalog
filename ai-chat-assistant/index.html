Good to know

Questions about the assistant.

Does it replace my staff?

No. It handles the first questions, the ones you answer ten times a day, and passes interested customers to you on WhatsApp with their name and number. You still close the deal.

What if it doesn't know the answer?

It only answers from the information you give us, like your services, prices and hours. If a question goes beyond that, it says so and offers to have you follow up on WhatsApp instead of guessing.

Which languages does it speak?

It replies in the language your customer writes in, including English, Arabic, Hindi, Urdu and many more, with no extra setup.

Where do the leads go?

Every chat that turns into a lead is sent to your inbox with the customer's name, WhatsApp number, what they want and the full conversation.

Can I change my prices or services later?

Yes. Updates to the assistant's information are covered by the monthly fee. Just message us what changed.

Do I need a Velorien website?

No. It can be added to most websites where a small piece of code can be placed. Tell us what your site is built on and we'll confirm.

How much does it cost?

Setup is AED 1,200, then AED 200 per month. If you're on Website Care, the monthly fee is AED 150, and setup is included with the Premium Website.
