Good to know

Questions about your website.

How much does a website cost in Dubai?

Our business websites start at AED 2,099 for a Starter landing page, AED 3,499 for a 4-page Professional website and AED 5,500 for a fully custom Premium website. Extra pages are AED 300 each, and Website Care is AED 500 per month if you want us to keep it updated.

How long does a website take?

Starter takes 7 days, Professional 10–14 days and Premium 14–21 days. The clock starts once we have your content and key details, so sending them early is the fastest way to launch.

What do I need to provide?

Your logo if you have one, the services or products you offer, your contact details and any photos you want used. Not sure what you have? Tell us on WhatsApp and we'll point out what's missing.

Are domain and hosting included?

Domain and hosting fees are paid separately to the provider. We can advise on what to buy and help connect it to your website at launch.

Will my website work on phones?

Yes. Every package is designed for mobile and desktop from the start, so it looks right and loads fast on the phone your customers are actually using.

Can I ask for changes?

Yes. Revisions are part of the design and build stages, so you see the work take shape and can steer it. The exact scope is confirmed when we agree your package.

Can you add an AI chat assistant to my website?

Yes. It answers your customers 24/7 in their language and sends you their WhatsApp number. Setup is AED 1,200, then AED 200 per month, and setup is included with the Premium Website. See how it works.
