Who you'll work with

Velorien is run by Hitin Banga, an independent web and UI/UX designer in Dubai. You deal directly with the person designing and building your site.

How much does a website cost in Dubai?

Our business websites start at AED 2,099 for a Starter landing page, AED 3,499 for a 4-page Professional website and AED 5,500 for a fully custom Premium website. Website Care is AED 500 per month and extra pages are AED 300 each.

How long does a website take?

Starter takes 7 days, Professional 10–14 days and Premium 14–21 days. The clock starts once we have your content and key details, so sending them early is the fastest way to launch.

What do I need to provide?

Your logo if you have one, the services or products you offer, your contact details and any photos you want used. Not sure what you have? Tell us on WhatsApp and we'll point out what's missing.

Are domain and hosting included?

Domain and hosting fees are paid separately to the provider. We can advise on what to buy and help connect it to your website at launch.

How does payment work?

We agree the payment schedule with you before any work starts, typically a deposit to begin and the balance at launch.

Can I ask for changes?

Yes. Revisions are part of the design and build stages, so you see the work take shape and can steer it. The exact scope is confirmed when we agree your package.

Can I add more pages later?

Yes. Extra pages are available from AED 300 each, or you can move up to a larger package. Starter is a single landing page with your services, so many clients add pages as they grow.

What happens after launch?

You get a finished website and a clear handover. If you'd like it kept updated, backed up and monitored, Website Care is available at AED 500 per month. It's optional.

Do you work with businesses outside Dubai?

Yes. We're based in Dubai and work with clients across the UAE and beyond. Everything runs over WhatsApp, email and calls.
